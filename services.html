Service FAQs

Good questions.

Which service should I start with?

Most businesses start with brand identity if they are new, or with SEO and social media if they already have a brand. On a free discovery call we recommend the order that gives the fastest return.

Can I combine services into one plan?

Yes. Our Laveesho Essential, Growth and Elite packages bundle social media content, Meta and Google Ads management and reporting. SEO, branding, websites and other services can be added as a custom plan.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO helps you rank in Google results. AEO helps your content appear as direct answers in snippets and voice search. GEO helps AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini cite your brand in generated answers.

Do you only work with businesses in Dubai?

We are based in Business Bay, Dubai and work with businesses across the UAE. Get in touch if you are elsewhere — much of our work is delivered remotely.

How do you report results?

Every plan includes a monthly report covering reach, engagement, rankings, leads and ad performance, plus what we will change next month.

Good to know.

Ad spend is separate

You pay Meta and Google advertising budgets directly, or as separately agreed. Package fees cover our agency management, not media spend.

Quoted separately

Full website development, mobile apps, influencer fees, models or actors, paid stock assets, additional shoot days, extra accounts and work outside the stated scope.

Scope in writing

Shoot duration, content formats, revisions, platforms and accounts, campaign limits and response times are confirmed in your proposal and contract.

No guaranteed results

We don't promise a fixed number of leads, sales or revenue. Results depend on your market, offer, budget, competition and how quickly enquiries are followed up.

Pricing FAQs

Clear costs.

How much does social media management cost in Dubai?

At Laveesho, social media management starts at AED 3,000 per month with Laveesho Essential: Instagram and Facebook, 8 static or carousel posts, 4 reels, 12 stories, Meta Ads management for 1 campaign, a monthly content calendar and a performance report.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Package fees cover agency management: strategy, setup, creative and optimisation. Your advertising budget is paid directly to Meta or Google, or as separately agreed, so you keep full control of spend.

Is there a minimum contract?

Plans are billed monthly. We recommend at least three months for SEO and ads, so there is enough data to optimise properly.

Can I upgrade or change plans later?

Yes. You can move between Essential, Growth and Elite as your business grows, or add custom services at any time.

What does AED 100 a day mean?

It's the daily equivalent of the monthly fee, based on a 30-day month, to make costs easy to compare. You're not billed daily: each package is billed monthly.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. We don't promise a fixed number of leads, sales or revenue, because results depend on your market, offer, budget, competition and follow-up. We set clear goals together and report on them every month.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are shown in UAE dirhams (AED) and exclude 5% VAT.

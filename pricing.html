Pricing FAQs

Clear costs.

How much does social media management cost in Dubai?

At Laveesho, social media management starts at AED 3,000 per month on the Launch plan, which includes 8 designed posts, management and a monthly report.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Plan prices cover strategy, setup, creative and management. Your advertising budget is paid directly to Meta or Google, so you keep full control of spend.

Is there a minimum contract?

Plans are billed monthly. We recommend at least three months for SEO and ads, so there is enough data to optimise properly.

Can I upgrade or change plans later?

Yes. You can move between Launch, Scale and Dominate as your business grows, or add custom services at any time.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are shown in UAE dirhams (AED) and exclude 5% VAT.

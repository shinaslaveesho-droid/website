What does Laveesho do?

Laveesho is a creative branding and digital marketing agency in Business Bay, Dubai. We offer brand identity, social media marketing, SEO and AI search optimisation, paid Meta and Google ads, website and e-commerce development, and app development.

How much does digital marketing cost in Dubai?

Our monthly packages are Laveesho Essential at AED 3,000, Laveesho Growth at AED 6,000 and Laveesho Elite at AED 12,000, billed monthly. Advertising budget is paid separately. See every inclusion on our pricing page, or ask for a custom plan.

What is GEO, and can you get my brand into AI answers?

GEO (generative engine optimisation) means structuring your website and content so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity can understand, trust and cite your brand. We combine it with classic SEO so you show up in both search results and AI answers.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO builds over time. Technical fixes and Google Business Profile improvements can show early movement within weeks, while competitive keyword rankings usually take several months of consistent work.

Do you build websites and apps as well as marketing?

Yes. Our team designs and develops websites, e-commerce stores and web or mobile apps, so your brand, platform and campaigns are built by one team.
